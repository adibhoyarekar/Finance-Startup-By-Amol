Bank Auctions

Flats, shops, plots, vehicles and gold sold by banks under SARFAESI – watch prices move live and bid from your phone with a verified EMD.

  • Complete KYC – PAN, Aadhaar e-KYC and the bank account for your EMD refund.
  • Pay EMD & apply – Usually 10% of the reserve price, paid before the last date.
  • Bid live – Bid in fixed steps or set a maximum and let auto-bid work for you.
  • Win & pay – Pay 25% by the next working day and the balance in 15 days.
