How bank auctions work

Four steps, all online. The bank verifies your EMD before you can bid, and losing bidders get their EMD back.

1Complete KYC

PAN, Aadhaar e-KYC and the bank account where your EMD refund should go.

2Pay EMD & apply

Usually 10% of the reserve price, paid by NEFT/RTGS before the last date. Book a site visit if you like.

3Bid live

Bid in fixed steps or set a maximum and let auto-bid work for you. Late bids extend the clock by 5 minutes.

4Win & pay

Pay 25% by the next working day and the balance in 15 days. The bank then issues the sale certificate.
