Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how RAVEN collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data when you use our website and services. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), RAVEN is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data we decide how to use.

Please read this policy together with our Terms & Conditions.

03

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and match you with the right loan, account, investment, property or auction.
  • To check your eligibility and recommend the best options for you. We may use automated tools and AI models to do this. The final decision is always the partner’s, and you can ask a person at RAVEN to review a recommendation.
  • To prepare and follow up on applications you ask us to support.
  • To verify identity, prevent fraud and meet legal requirements such as KYC.
  • To keep the website secure, fix problems and improve how it works.
  • To send service updates you asked for. We send marketing only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
04

Consent and your control

We use your personal data based on the consent you give us, or for other legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act, such as meeting a legal duty. Each consent request tells you what data we need and why.

You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by writing to us. Withdrawing does not affect what was done before, and some services may stop working without the data they need.

05

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share only what is needed, with:

  • Banks, NBFCs, insurers, mutual fund platforms and other partners: only the details needed for a request you have asked us to handle, with your consent.
  • Licensed credit bureaus (such as TransUnion CIBIL, Experian, Equifax or CRIF High Mark): only if you ask us to check your credit score or eligibility.
  • Technology providers, including hosting and AI model providers, that help us run RAVEN. They process data only on our instructions.
  • Government or law-enforcement authorities, when the law requires it.
06

Credit information

If you ask us to check your credit score, we will fetch your credit report from a licensed credit bureau with your explicit consent. A score check you request is a soft enquiry, so it does not lower your credit score.

We use your credit report only for the purpose you agreed to, such as showing your score and finding loans you are likely to qualify for.

In plain words: checking your own score through RAVEN does not affect it.

07

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage to remember choices, such as your acceptance of these terms, and to keep demo dashboards working on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can clear this data at any time in your browser settings.

08

Keeping your data safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and access is limited to people who need it to serve you.

No system is completely secure, so please keep your password and documents private. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

09

How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as it is needed to provide our services, meet legal and accounting requirements (including KYC record-keeping) and resolve disputes. After that, it is deleted or made anonymous.

10

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed on secure cloud servers, which may be in India or abroad. Any transfer outside India follows the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

11

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: get a summary of your data and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct and update: fix data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Erase: ask us to delete data we no longer need.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, for future use.
  • Raise a grievance: and get a response from us.
  • Nominate: someone to use these rights for you in case of death or incapacity.

To use any of these rights, write to hello@raven.business. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

12

Children

RAVEN is meant for adults and business owners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

13

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will let you know and, where needed, ask for your consent again.

Questions or a complaint?

Our Grievance Desk is here to help. We acknowledge every request within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 15 days.
