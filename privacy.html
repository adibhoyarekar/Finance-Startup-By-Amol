Who we are

RAVEN is a business partner for entrepreneurs in India, offering support with funding and loans, insurance, real estate guidance and bank auctions. This policy explains how RAVEN collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use this website.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, business details and the message you write in our contact, enquiry and registration forms.
  • Account details: a username and password if you create a RAVEN account once accounts are available.
  • Chat questions: the questions you type into the Ask a question assistant, once it is switched on.
  • Basic technical data: your browser type, device and the pages you visit, which helps us keep the site working well.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and match you with the right loan, insurance, real estate or auction service.
  • To prepare and follow up on applications you ask us to support.
  • To keep the website secure, fix problems and improve how it works.
  • To send service updates you have asked for. We will not send marketing without your consent.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. With your permission, we may share the details needed for your request with the banks, lenders, insurers and other partners that handle it. Service providers who help us run the website may also process data on our behalf, and only for that purpose. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

The website stores a small note in your browser to remember that you accepted our Terms & Conditions, so we do not ask you again on every visit. You can clear this at any time from your browser settings, and you will then be asked again.

Keeping your data safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transfer or storage is completely secure, so please keep your password and documents confidential.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as it is needed to provide our services, meet legal and accounting requirements and resolve disputes. After that it is deleted or made anonymous.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Please write to us using the contact details below and we will respond within a reasonable time, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

This website is meant for adults and business owners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version, and continued use of the website means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Write to hello@raven.business or use our Contact page. RAVEN, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar & Pune, India.
